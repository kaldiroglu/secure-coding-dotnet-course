# Code of Conduct

*For further enquiry please contact Akin Kaldiroglu at akin@kaldiroglu.dev*

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of conduct.
The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in this course and its community a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background
or identity.

## Our standards
Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment includes:
- Being respectful, patient, and considerate toward other learners.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for learning.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and
any conduct a reasonable person would consider unprofessional or unwelcome.
Maintainers may remove or reject contributions, comments, or participants that
violate this code.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, and
discussions — and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting & enforcement
Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, **Akin Kaldiroglu**, at
**akin@kaldiroglu.dev**. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially and
fairly. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take
appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem
inappropriate.
